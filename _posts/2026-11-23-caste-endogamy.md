I have occasionally heard people ask if caste really matters today. The answer, of course, is yes. And I want to talk about one particular dimension of it, which is the role it plays in choosing who we marry.

When looking for data on it, I found [this article](https://scroll.in/article/807948/just-5-of-marriages-in-india-are-inter-caste-says-report), that talks about a survey from 2011-2012 found that close to 95% of people in India married from their own caste. Think of that astounding number. That means, if you talk to 20 people 19 of them would have married into the same caste.

I would love to see a more modern number, but I am not able to find any newer studies. So, I went looking for this dataset and found it [here](https://www.icpsr.umich.edu/web/DSDR/studies/36151/versions/V6). The website also has a way to do cross tabs right there without having to download the data, and here is what I found.

The survey, among many other things, asks women if their husband is from the same caste as theirs or not, and here is the breakdown. Of 39,245 surveyed 37,290 (95.01%) said yes and 1,955 (4.9%) said no.

When I broke the data down by caste, it looks pretty uniform across all the caste categories.

|Caste Category|No|Yes|%No|%Yes|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Brahmin|109|1883|5%|95%|
|Forward/General (except Brahmin)|573|8604|6%|94%|
|Other Backward Castes (OBC)|733|15223|5%|95%|
|Scheduled Castes (SC)|346|8021|4%|96%|
|Scheduled Tribes (ST)|173|3059|5%|95%|
|Others|21|500|4%|96%|

Note that while the caste breakdown categories are collections of many different castes, that does not necessarily mean that "Yes" means they were mariied to the person in the same category. The yes, I *think* indicates that they were married to a person of the same caste (not just in the same category) because the data point is described as "Husband's family same caste as your natal family", and I would think people would not answer it as "Yes" if both are, say, from the OBC category.

My own home state of Tamil Nadu aligns with the national average. One might have thought that better educated states (like the ones in the south) might have more inter-caste marriages, but this only goes to show how strong the hold of caste is on Indian minds. So the next time someone asks if caste is still a big deal, show them this.
